# Bluewater Bank: cards (fictional demo company)

Block a card: if a card is lost or stolen, block it at once through the 24x7 helpline or in the app under Cards > Block card. A blocked card cannot be unblocked; a replacement card is sent within 5 working days.

Card not working or declined: check that the card is not expired, blocked or over its limit, and that the PIN is correct. Online payments can also fail if online use is switched off in the app.

Card security: never share the full card number, CVV, PIN or one-time passwords, including with bank staff or chat assistants.
