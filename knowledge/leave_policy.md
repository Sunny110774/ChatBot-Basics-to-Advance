# Bluewater Bank: leave policy for employees (fictional demo company)

Notice: planned leave must be requested at least 5 working days in advance where possible.

Approval: leave is approved by the employee's manager and depends on the available balance and team cover. Leave is not confirmed until the manager approves it in the HR portal.

How to apply: open the HR portal, go to Leave > New request, choose the dates and leave type, and submit for manager approval.

Leave balance: employees see their remaining balance in the HR portal under Leave > Balance. A chatbot cannot confirm a balance or approval without a response from the HR system.
