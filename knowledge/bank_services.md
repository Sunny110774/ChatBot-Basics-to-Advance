# Bluewater Bank: banking hours and branches (fictional demo company)

Banking hours: Bluewater Bank branches are open Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 5 PM. Online and mobile banking are available 24x7.

Holidays: branches are closed on weekends and national public holidays. Online banking stays available on holidays.

Same-day payment cut-off: payments submitted before 2 PM on a working day are processed the same day. Payments submitted after 2 PM, or on a weekend or holiday, are processed on the next working day.

Branches and ATMs: customers find the nearest branch or ATM with the branch locator on the Bluewater Bank website or app, by entering their PIN code.

ATM withdrawal limit: the daily ATM cash withdrawal limit is 25,000 per card.
